### (2) Student-Friendly Neighborhoods for Pólo II & Transit Connections

Coimbra is famous for its steep hills, meaning transit connectivity and topography are critical factors when choosing a room. Pólo II is situated south of the city center in Pinhal de Marrocos. The following neighborhoods offer the best balance of proximity, amenities, and transit:

#### Recommended Neighborhoods

1. **Pinhal de Marrocos / Quinta da Portela**
   * **Commute to Pólo II:** Immediate walking distance (**5–12 minutes** on foot).
   * **Profile:** Modern, quiet residential expansions directly adjacent to the engineering departments, research institutes (e.g., Instituto Pedro Nunes), and SASUC Pólo II halls.
   * **Amenities:** Local student cafes, university canteens, green parks, and direct river access. Ideal for students who prioritize zero daily transit time.
2. **Vale das Flores**
   * **Commute to Pólo II:** **10–15 minutes** walking or 5 minutes by bicycle/bus.
   * **Profile:** One of Coimbra’s most vibrant and practical student hubs, located right between Pólo II and ISEC.
   * **Amenities:** Home to **CoimbraShopping** (large Continente hypermarket, retail stores, food court), multiple gyms (Fitness Hut, Solinca), 24-hour pharmacies, laundromats, and traditional bakeries along Rua Paulo Quintela.
3. **Bairro Norton de Matos (BNM)**
   * **Commute to Pólo II:** **15–20 minutes** on foot or ~7 minutes by bus.
   * **Profile:** Historic garden-style residential quarter known for friendly community living, authentic Portuguese tascas, and relaxed flat terrain.
   * **Amenities:** Norton de Matos municipal fresh produce market, local grocery shops, cultural centers, and lower average rental rates than downtown.
4. **Solum**
   * **Commute to Pólo II:** **20–25 minutes** on foot or ~10 minutes by direct bus.
   * **Profile:** Eastern Coimbra’s primary commercial and recreational district around the Estádio Cidade de Coimbra.
   * **Amenities:** **Alma Shopping** (Auchan hypermarket, cinema, dining), municipal Olympic swimming pools, banks, medical clinics, and lively student bars.

#### Direct SMTUC Public Transit Lines Serving Pólo II

The municipal public bus operator (**SMTUC — Serviços Municipalizados de Transportes Urbanos de Coimbra**) runs key direct corridors connecting Pólo II with these neighborhoods and the city center:

* **Linha 34 (Universidade Pólo I ⇄ Pólo II ⇄ Quinta da Portela):** The primary academic trunk line connecting the historic Alta campus (Pólo I) directly to Pólo II, Vale das Flores, and Quinta da Portela. High departure frequency on class days.
* **Linha 38 / 38F / 38T (Santa Clara ⇄ Portagem ⇄ Pólo II ⇄ Vale das Flores / Quinta da Portela):** Essential route linking downtown (Largo da Portagem/Baixa), Santa Clara, Pólo II, and Vale das Flores.
* **Linha 54 (Portagem ⇄ Pólo II ⇄ Portela):** Fast transit corridor connecting the central Portagem transit interchange directly to Pólo II.
* **Linha 37 (Vale das Flores ⇄ Solum ⇄ Celas / Hospitais HUC):** Connects the Vale das Flores/Pólo II area to Solum and the northern healthcare campus at Pólo III.

> **Student Transit Benefit (*Passe Jovens Estudantes*):** Under Portugal’s nationwide transit policy, higher education students up to age 23 (or up to 24–26 for specific degree tracks) are entitled to the **free SMTUC monthly student transit pass** (*Sub23 / Gratuito*). Upon arriving and completing UC enrollment, take your enrollment proof (*Declaração de Matrícula*) to an SMTUC office (e.g., at Loja do Cidadão or Praça da República) to activate unlimited free municipal bus travel.

### (4) Legal Regulations, Deposits, NIF/Receipts & Scam Prevention

#### Portuguese Rental Law & Deposit Caps
* **Written Contract (*Contrato de Arrendamento*):** Under Portuguese tenancy legislation (*Novo Regime do Arrendamento Urbano* – NRAU and Article 1069.º of the Portuguese Civil Code), residential rental agreements must be concluded in writing. Ensure your contract specifies the monthly rent, whether bills are included (and any usage limits, e.g., "bills included up to €50/person"), the exact lease duration, and deposit return terms.
* **Security Deposit Limits (*Caução*):** Under Article 1076.º of the Portuguese Civil Code, a security deposit is legally capped at a **maximum of 2 months’ rent**. In student flatshares, standard practice is **1 month’s deposit** plus the 1st month's rent. Landlords are legally prohibited from demanding unreasonable upfront sums (such as 6–12 months paid in advance). The deposit must be refunded at lease end after a check-out room inspection.

#### NIF and Official Electronic Rent Receipts (*Recibos de Renda Eletrónicos*)
* **NIF (*Número de Identificação Fiscal*):** Every tenant in Portugal is strongly advised to obtain a Portuguese tax number. A NIF can be requested free of charge or for a nominal fee at any local tax office (*Serviço de Finanças*) or *Loja do Cidadão* with your passport/national ID and proof of address.
* **Electronic Tax Receipts:** Landlords are legally mandated to register the lease on the Portuguese Tax Authority portal (*Portal das Finanças*) and issue official monthly electronic rent receipts (*Recibos de Renda Eletrónicos*). These official receipts protect your legal tenancy status and serve as verified proof of accommodation for your home university’s Erasmus+ grant compliance and administrative requirements.

#### Warning Signs & Rental Scams to Avoid
* **The "Abroad Landlord" Pretext:** Be cautious of individuals claiming they are currently abroad (e.g., working for international organizations, missionary work, or military service) and offering to courier keys once you wire funds. Legitimate landlords either manage properties through verified portals or arrange verified local agents.
* **Untraceable Wire Transfers & Fake Links:** Never send security deposits or rent via Western Union, MoneyGram, Ria, or cryptocurrency. Be alert to spoofed email links or fraudulent payment websites that mimic Uniplaces or Airbnb. Always complete bookings and communications within the official platform until confirmed.
* **Prices "Too Good to Be True":** Be skeptical of newly renovated luxury studios or grand central apartments advertised for €200–€280/month. Standard Coimbra studio prices start above €500.
* **Pressure for Cash Deals Without Contracts:** Refuse requests to conduct rent payments strictly under the table in cash without written agreements or receipts; this leaves you with no legal protections, no recourse if evicted, and no proof of housing for university or visa requirements.

With these guidelines, target neighborhoods like **Vale das Flores, Pinhal de Marrocos, or Solum**, reserve your room during the **July 2026** peak window, and you will secure an ideal shared flat near Pólo II well within your €500 budget!

## Commute and Public Transportation in Coimbra

Welcome to Coimbra! ### 1. Public Transport Network
Coimbra's urban and regional transit network connects all major residential student neighborhoods (such as Baixa, Celas, Olivais, and Santa Clara) to the university's main hubs (Polo I in the historic center, Polo II engineering campus, and Polo III health sciences campus).

* **SMTUC (*Serviços Municipalizados de Transportes Urbanos de Coimbra*):** The backbone of urban mobility in Coimbra. SMTUC operates an extensive network of buses that link residential areas directly to university faculties and student residences. 
  * Official Website: [smtuc.pt](https://www.smtuc.pt/)
* **Metro Mondego:** The newly integrated light rail/Metrobus transit system linking regional and urban corridors (such as Coimbra-B station, Praça da República, and suburban lines toward Lousã/Serpins).
  * Official Website: [metromondego.pt](https://www.metromondego.pt/)
* **CP (*Comboios de Portugal*):** Provides national and regional suburban train services, connecting Coimbra and Coimbra-B stations to major hubs like Porto and Lisbon.
  * Official Website: [cp.pt](https://www.cp.pt/)

---

### 2. Student Passes & Discounts
Public transport fares in Coimbra are heavily subsidized for youth and students under municipal and national social tariff frameworks.

* **Passe Gratuito Jovem (Free Youth Pass / Sub-24):**
  * **Eligibility:** Available to young people up to 24 years old (inclusive) residing or studying in Portugal, which covers the vast majority of incoming Erasmus undergraduates.
  * **Price:** **€0.00 / month** (completely free for unlimited urban bus travel on SMTUC network).
  * **Required Documents:** Official identification document (Citizen Card, EU National ID, or Passport) and proof of enrollment at Universidade de Coimbra.
* **Standard Student Pass (Ages 25 and older):**
  * **Eligibility:** Enrolled students at Universidade de Coimbra who are 25 years of age or older.
  * **Price:** Approximately **€15.00 / month** for standard urban student tariff passes.
  * **Required Documents:** UC student enrollment certificate and official ID.
* **Application & Loading:** Passes can be requested and loaded physically at SMTUC customer service desks (*Loja SMTUC*, *Loja de S. José*, *Loja do Mercado*) or digitally via the [SMTUC Online Portal](https://online.smtuc.pt/SITE).

### 3. Cycling & Alternative Commute
Coimbra's hilly terrain presents a unique mix of flat riverfront paths and steep inclines up to Polo I. 

* **UCicletas:** Managed by *Desporto UC* (UC Sports), **UCicletas** is the official bicycle-sharing and temporary lending program for the UC academic community. It allows students to borrow bicycles for semester-long or academic year use to commute across campus areas sustainably.
  * Official Information: [UCicletas Program](https://www.uc.pt/en/uc-sports/ucicletas/)
* **Walking Tips:** 
  * Walking from **Baixa** (downtown) to **Polo I** involves climbing the historic *Almedina* stairs or taking the *Elevador do Mercado* (public elevator connecting lower and middle levels).
  * Electric scooters and shared micro-mobility options are widely available throughout the city center for quick hops.
